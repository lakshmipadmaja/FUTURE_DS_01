# Key Business Insights

## Business Sales Performance Analytics

### 1. Overall Performance
- Total Revenue: $74.97 Million
- Total Profit: $29.56 Million
- Total Orders: 61,099
- Total Customers: 14,680
- Average Order Value: Approximately $1,227
- Profit Margin: 39.43%

### 2. Regional Performance
- South region recorded the highest revenue and profit.
- Focus on improving sales performance in other regions.

### 3. Product Performance
- Electronics generated the highest revenue.
- Grocery achieved the highest profit margin at 52.44%.

### 4. Customer Insights
- Repeat customers contributed 98.39% of revenue.
- Repeat purchase rate was 93.11%.

### 5. Marketing Insights
- Organic Search recorded the highest channel revenue.

### 6. Delivery and Returns
- Delivery delay rate: 14.87%.
- Overall return rate: 6.85%.
- Average customer rating: 3.68.

### Conclusion
The analysis highlights strong repeat-customer contribution, regional sales differences, and opportunities to improve delivery performance and product profitability.

*Note: Financial KPIs are based on USD records. Marketing ROI could not be calculated because advertising spend data was unavailable.*
